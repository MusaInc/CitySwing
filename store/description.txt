CitySwing is about one thing: the thrill of moving through a city at speed.

Fire a web at any tower, swing through the bottom of the arc, let go at the top and fly. Chain swings between skyscrapers, run along a facade, scramble over a ledge and launch off again. The physics are real — momentum is earned, a perfect release flings you higher, and a mistimed line puts you into a wall.

A WHOLE CITY
• A coastal city to explore: a downtown of glass towers, an animated entertainment district, brick neighbourhoods, a waterfront, beaches, a marina and a mainland across the strait
• Ride the City Loop trains, swing under the bridges, climb the tallest towers for the view
• Day and night cycle and changing weather — clear skies, sunsets, fog, rain and storms

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Open City — go anywhere, collect 40 hidden tokens, take on jobs
• Time Trial — 8 courses through the city, bronze, silver and gold medals
• Endurance — stay in the air as long as you can
• Training — learn the moves step by step

JOBS AND A LIFE IN THE CITY
• Rooftop deliveries, rescues and surveys for honest pay — or riskier work for the Fence, with drones on your tail
• Earn coins and buy five homes, from a bedsit to a penthouse, each a real floor of a real tower: swing in through the window, sleep, change clothes, see your trophies
• Make the courier your own: suits, hair, tops, trousers, shoes, gloves, masks, web colours and four gadgets (jet boots, glider, chrono watch, coin magnet)

MADE FOR TOUCH
• Move with the left thumb, jump and swing with the right; the aim assist picks your line, or switch on tap-to-aim in Settings and tap the exact building you want
• Drag anywhere on the right to look around
• Graphics quality options and a built-in performance test
