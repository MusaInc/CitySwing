CITYSWING — A GAME SHAPED BY ITS PLAYERS

Swing through an open city of skyscrapers, rooftops and bridges. Build momentum, chase speed, explore freely and find your own way around.

But CitySwing is about more than swinging.

I built the game. You decide where it goes next.

Hi, I'm the independent developer behind CitySwing.

I started this project because I wanted to create a swinging game that feels genuinely fun to play. No ads, no interruptions. Just a city, your movement and the freedom to explore.

But I don't want to spend the next few years deciding everything on my own.

I want you to help build CitySwing.

Have an idea for a new feature? Think the swinging could feel better? Want different buildings, challenges, locations, outfits or ways to explore?

Tell me in your App Store review.

I'll personally read your feedback and work to reply with a real answer. If your idea is going into a future update, I'll tell you. If I'm considering it, I'll say so. And if I don't think it'll work, I'll explain why.

Not every suggestion will make it into the game, but every thoughtful suggestion deserves consideration.

You don't have to leave five stars. I want your genuine opinion, including what needs fixing.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Swing through a sprawling city of skyscrapers and bridges.
• Build speed, chain swings and master your movement.
• Explore rooftops, streets and waterfronts.
• Take on challenges and discover your favourite routes.
• Experience the freedom of an open-world swinging game.

THIS IS JUST THE BEGINNING

Every update is an opportunity to make CitySwing better, and I want the people actually playing it to help decide what comes next.

The review section isn't just somewhere to leave a rating. I want it to become a place where ideas are shared, improvements are discussed and players can see their feedback making a difference.

I'm not here to tell you what CitySwing should become.

I'm here to build it with you.

Your ideas. My code. Our game.

— The developer behind CitySwing
