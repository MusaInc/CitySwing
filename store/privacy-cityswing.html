CitySwing privacy policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

CitySwing is a single-player game for iPhone and iPad made by Musa Studios.

What we collect: nothing. The game has no account or login, does not send anything over the internet, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Your game progress and settings (coins, unlocks, homes, best times and preferences) are stored only on your device, by the game itself. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Links: the Character credits screen has two links to licence and asset pages; they open in your web browser only if you tap them.

Children: the game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes: if a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact: Musa Studios, support@musastudios.co.uk
