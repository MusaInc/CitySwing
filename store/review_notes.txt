2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
CitySwing is a single-player, skill-based movement game. The player fires a rope ("web") at buildings and swings through a fictional coastal city, building momentum, chaining swings and landing on rooftops. It is for anyone who enjoys movement, parkour and racing games (rated 4+; there is no blood, gore, combat or online interaction). Its value is a physics-driven traversal game that is fully playable offline, with no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. It is a paid app.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, demo account or sample file is needed. Launch the app; after the splash, the main menu offers:
- PLAY (Open City): free swinging across the whole city, with optional Job Board contracts, collectible tokens and Homes.
- Training: a step-by-step tutorial (recommended first).
- Time Trial: checkpoint courses with medals.
- Endurance: stay airborne as long as possible.
- Shop, Homes, Progress, Settings.
Controls (landscape, iPhone and iPad): the left half of the screen is a movement stick. On the right, hold the large SWING button to fire and hold a web; lift your finger to let go. JUMP jumps. Drag elsewhere on the right side to look around. The pause button is at the top right. Coins are earned only by playing and are spent in the Shop and Homes; they cannot be bought with real money. Progress saves automatically on the device.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. CitySwing makes no network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services, and no third-party SDKs. It is built with the open-source Godot Engine and Jolt Physics (both MIT licensed), compiled into the app. The only external action is two optional links in Settings > Character credits, which open public licence pages in Safari.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app's features and content are identical in every region. The game is in English.
