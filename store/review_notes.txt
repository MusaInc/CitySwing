CitySwing is a single-player game with no account, no network features and no in-app purchases. To try it: tap PLAY (Open City). Move with the left side of the screen; on the right, the large SWING button fires the web (hold to stay on it, lift the finger to let go) and JUMP jumps. Drag anywhere else on the right side to look around. Training (main menu) teaches the controls step by step. Time Trial and Endurance are on the main menu. Pause (top right) has Homes, the Job Board and Settings. No login or demo account is needed.
