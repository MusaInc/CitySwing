Response to Guideline 2.1 (Performance: App Completeness – the app crashed after the initial launch), submission 5f3041e4-2e3b-4ba3-8124-f447e2ffd6de:

WHAT WE FIXED IN BUILD 56
On a fresh install, build 52 compiled its graphics shaders the first time each effect appeared during play and drew the 3D city at close to full panel resolution. On some devices this produced long stalls shortly after launch, which the system could end as a crash. Build 56 (version 1.0.0):
- compiles those shaders while the loading screen is still showing, so nothing stalls during play;
- renders the 3D view at a fixed pixel budget with MetalFX upscaling (the HUD stays at native resolution), and lowers 3D resolution automatically if a device cannot hold its frame rate;
- uses cheaper shadow filtering on iPhone and iPad.
We re-tested a fresh install of build 56 on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS: it launches, loads and plays smoothly, including relaunching the app. The camera was also brought closer to the player.

The answers to the earlier information request (6 November) follow and still apply. The attached screen recording was made with an earlier build; the launch flow, menus and controls are unchanged.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
CitySwing is a single-player, skill-based movement game. The player fires a rope ("web") at buildings and swings through a fictional coastal city, building momentum, chaining swings and landing on rooftops. It is for anyone who enjoys movement, parkour and racing games (rated 4+; there is no blood, gore, combat or online interaction). Its value is a physics-driven traversal game that is fully playable offline, with no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. It is a paid app.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login or sample files. After the splash, the main menu offers PLAY (Open City), Training (tutorial, recommended first), Time Trial, Endurance, Shop, Homes, Progress and Settings. Controls (landscape): left half = movement stick; hold SWING to fire and hold a web, release to let go; JUMP jumps; drag on the right to look; pause at top right. Coins are earned only by playing (no real-money purchases). Progress saves on the device.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. CitySwing makes no network requests and includes no analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services, and no third-party SDKs. Built with the open-source Godot Engine and Jolt Physics (MIT). Settings > Character credits has two optional links to public licence pages.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app's features and content are identical in every region. The game is in English.
